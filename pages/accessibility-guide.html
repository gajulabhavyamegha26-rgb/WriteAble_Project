Accessibility Guide

WriteAble is designed so that more people can write comfortably. This page explains the tools available and how to use them.

The Accessibility button

Every page has an Accessibility button at the bottom right. Open it to find these options:

  • Text size: choose A- or A+ to make text smaller or larger (100%, 115%, 130% or 150%). Reset returns to normal.
  • Theme: Light, Dark, or High contrast (black background with bright text).
  • Wider spacing: adds extra space between letters, words and lines, which many readers find easier.
  • Read aloud: reads your text out loud. In the Editor it reads what you have written (or only the part you have selected). On other pages it reads the page content. Choose it again to stop.

Your choices are saved in your browser, so they stay the same on every page and the next time you visit.

Voice input

On the Editor page, choose Start speaking and talk. Your words are added to the text box. Choose Stop listening when you are finished.

  • Voice input works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.
  • Your browser will ask for permission to use the microphone. Choose Allow.
  • You need an internet connection.

Using the keyboard

  • Tab moves to the next link or button. Shift + Tab moves back.
  • Enter or Space activates a button.
  • The first Tab on any page shows a Skip to main content link that jumps past the menu.
  • Esc closes the Accessibility panel.

Screen readers

Pages use headings, landmarks and labelled buttons so screen readers can move around quickly. Messages such as "Checking your writing..." and the number of suggestions are announced automatically.

Other helpful tips

  • Use your browser zoom (Ctrl and + on Windows, Cmd and + on Mac) for an even larger view.
  • The voice used for Read aloud comes from your device. You can change it in your device's settings.
  • If something does not work on your browser, try Chrome or Edge.
