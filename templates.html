Templates

Select a template, copy it, and paste it into the Editor. Replace everything in [square brackets] with your own details.

Formal email

Subject: [Clear topic, for example: Request for extension on Assignment 2]

Dear [Name],

I am writing to [reason for your email].

[Key details: what, when and why.]

[What you would like the reader to do, and by when.]

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[Your class or contact details]

Letter of application

[Your name]
[Your address]
[Date]

[Recipient's name and title]
[Organisation]

Dear [Name],

Subject: Application for [position or request]

I am a [year and course] student at [college]. I am writing to apply for [position or request].

[Paragraph 2: your skills, experience or reasons.]

[Paragraph 3: when you are available and what you hope happens next.]

Thank you for considering my application. I have attached [documents].

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]
[Phone number and email]

[What should happen next.]

Essay outline

Introduction
- Opening sentence that gets the reader's attention
- Background the reader needs
- Your main argument (thesis)

Body paragraph 1
- Point:
- Evidence:
- Explanation of how the evidence supports your point:

Body paragraph 2
- Point:
- Evidence:
- Explanation of how the evidence supports your point:

Body paragraph 3
- Point:
- Evidence:
- Explanation of how the evidence supports your point:

Conclusion
- Restate your main argument in new words
- Sum up your key points
- Finish with a final thought
